# Tauross 1.0.1

- Mirror multiple devices at once in separate native macOS windows, with independent close, minimize, and zoom controls. Clicking Mirror again restores the device's existing window.
- Add a companion toolbar with Android power, volume, navigation, rotation, and notifications, plus screenshot and video recording controls for both platforms.
- Save screenshots and recordings in `~/.tauross`, with separate Android and iOS recording settings for resolution, frame rate, bitrate, H.264 or HEVC encoding, and MP4 or MOV files.
- Share Android capture between mirroring and recording to avoid running a second device encoder and decoder. Keep the original mirror renderer and remove Show touches.
- Close Settings after Apply & Restart, restarting only the named device's mirror.
- Finish active recordings when closing a mirror or quitting Tauross.

## Current limits

- iOS capture and recording still need validation on a physical iPhone/iPad. iOS remains USB and view-only, with automatic source quality.
- Recordings contain video only; audio forwarding and iOS Wi-Fi are not included.
- This build is ad hoc signed and is not Apple notarized. Sparkle update feeds and archives are signed with Tauross's dedicated Ed25519 key.
